한국어

  1. 서비스: 우리집요정은 집 안 물건이 어디 있는지 적어 두고 찾는 앱입니다. 계정 없이 쓸 수 있고, 카카오 또는 Google 로 로그인하면 가족과 함께 쓰는 집과 요정 기능을 쓸 수 있습니다.
  2. 계정: 로그인한 계정은 본인만 씁니다. 계정은 앱의 설정 → 데이터 → 계정 삭제로 언제든 지울 수 있습니다.
  3. 가족과 함께 쓰는 집: 초대 코드를 받은 사람은 그 집의 물건 · 사진 · 기록을 보고 바꿀 수 있습니다. 초대 코드는 믿는 사람에게만 보내 주세요. 무료로는 두 사람까지 함께 쓸 수 있습니다.

English

  1. The service: Home Fairy is an app for noting and finding where things are at home. It works without an account; signing in with Kakao or Google adds a home shared with family and the fairy features.
  2. Accounts: an account is for its owner only. You can delete it at any time in Settings → Data → Delete my account.
  3. A shared home: anyone who joins with an invite code can see and change that home's things, photos and history. Send invite codes only to people you trust. Two people can share a home for free.
  4. Fairy features and credits: searching by description, adding from a photo and tidying a receipt use free monthly credits. The amount and the features may change; changes are announced in the app or on this page. There are no paid purchases at present.
  5. What you write: the things, photos and history you add are yours. The developer handles them only as the privacy policy says and uses them for nothing else.
  6. Please don't: use someone else's account, disrupt the service, call the fairy features in automated bulk, or share content that is illegal or harms others' rights.
  7. The fairy's answers: kinds, search matches, receipt lines and photo guesses are automatic and can be wrong; check them before saving. Recipes and barcode data are public data from the Korean Ministry of Food and Drug Safety; check allergies and health matters yourself.
  8. Limits of liability: the app is provided as is. To the extent the law allows, the developer is not liable for reminders that arrive late or not at all because of device settings or connectivity, or for the accuracy of what users write. Keep important records in a backup file too.
  9. Changes to the service: features may change or end. Server features are announced 30 days before they end, and records on the phone can always be moved out with a backup file.
  10. Changes to these terms: changes are posted on this page with their effective date. Continuing to use the app after a change means accepting the new terms.
  11. Governing law: the laws of the Republic of Korea.
