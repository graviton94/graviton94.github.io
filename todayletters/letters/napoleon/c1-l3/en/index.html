Napoleon to Joséphine · 7 April 1796

Letters from Napoleon · Chapter I To Italy · Albenga

Listen to the first sentence (French)

The letter and its translation

Je reçois une lettre que tu interromps pour aller, dis-tu, à la campagne ; et après cela tu te donnes le ton d'être jalouse de moi, qui suis ici accablé d'affaires et de fatigue.

I get a letter that you break off to go, you say, to the country; and after that you put on a show of being jealous of me, when I am here worn out with work and tiredness.

Je ne suis pas content : ta dernière lettre est froide comme l'amitié.

I'm not happy: your last letter is as cold as friendship.

Mon frère est ici. Il a appris mon mariage avec plaisir ; il brûle de l'envie de te connaître.

My brother is here. He was glad to hear of my marriage; he's longing to meet you.

Je cherche à le décider à venir à Paris. Sa femme a accouché : elle a fait une fille.

I'm trying to persuade him to come to Paris. His wife has given birth: she's had a girl.

Il t'envoie pour présent une boîte de bonbons de Gênes. Tu recevras des oranges, des parfums et de l'eau de fleur d'oranger que je t'envoie.

He's sending you a box of sweets from Genoa as a present. You'll also get the oranges, perfumes and orange-flower water that I'm sending you.

Selected sentences from the letter. The translation is our own.

Curator’s note
Two days later, still at Albenga. Joséphine had stayed in Paris, and her letters were rare and short. He grumbles that lukewarm ‘friendship’ is cold next to love. The ‘brother’ is Joseph, who happened to be in Italy; his wife Julie had just had a daughter. Genoa was a port famous for its candied fruit and sweets.
