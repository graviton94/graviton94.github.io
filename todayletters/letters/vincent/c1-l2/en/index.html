Vincent to Theo · 16 March 1888

Letters from Vincent · Chapter I The South · Arles

Listen to the first sentence (French)

The letter and its translation

Pour ce qui est du travail, j'ai rapporté une toile de 15 aujourd'hui, c'est un pont-levis sur lequel passe une petite voiture, qui se profile sur un ciel bleu.

As for work, today I brought back a size 15 canvas: a drawbridge with a little carriage going over it, outlined against a blue sky.

La rivière bleue également, des berges orangées avec verdure, un groupe de laveuses aux caracos et bonnets bariolés.

The river blue as well, orange banks with greenery, a group of washerwomen in bright smocks and bonnets.

Mais, mon cher frère — tu sais je me sens au Japon — je ne te dis que cela et encore je n'ai encore rien vu dans la splendeur accoutumée.

But, my dear brother — you know, I feel I'm in Japan — that's all I'll say, and I haven't yet seen anything in its usual splendour.

Ici je vois du neuf, j'apprends, et étant traité avec un peu de douceur, mon corps ne me refuse pas ses services.

Here I see new things, I learn, and treated with a little gentleness, my body doesn't refuse to serve me.

Selected sentences from the letter. The translation is our own.

Curator’s note
The Langlois bridge was a drawbridge on the canal south of Arles, named after its keeper. Vincent drew and painted it several times; the picture here is one of his pen drawings of it.
